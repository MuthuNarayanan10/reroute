How ReRoute works

A failed delivery is a sale looking for a buyer.

Somewhere near every refused parcel, someone left that exact product in their cart. ReRoute connects the two before the courier sends it back.

  1. Delivery fails

    The courier reports an NDR — refused, unreachable, door locked. ReRoute hears it in seconds.

  2. We find a buyer nearby

    Shoppers within 10 km with that product in their cart are ranked by distance, freshness and fit.

  3. They pay upfront

    A WhatsApp offer with a UPI link. The first payer wins; anyone else is refunded automatically.

  4. Delivered tomorrow

    The courier redirects the parcel, a new paid order is created, the old one closes. You keep the sale.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask.

Does it work with my courier?

Today ReRoute works with Shiprocket, which covers most Indian courier networks. Redirecting a parcel to a new buyer needs consignee-change support on your account. We help you set this up during onboarding.

What happens to the original customer?

Nothing is charged. ReRoute only rescues unpaid COD parcels that the customer refused or couldn’t receive. The original order is cancelled, and the parcel goes to someone who paid upfront.

What if two buyers pay for the same parcel?

The first valid payment wins. Every other offer link is cancelled, and anyone who paid late is refunded automatically through Razorpay.

Do I need Shopify?

Shopify is supported today. WooCommerce and custom websites are next, then Amazon and Flipkart for listing and inventory sync.

Is my customers’ data safe?

Yes. Store credentials are encrypted, every store’s data is isolated, and offers only go to shoppers who opted in to WhatsApp. Customers can ask for their data to be deleted at any time.

How long does setup take?

Connecting Shopify takes about two minutes. Adding your courier and Razorpay webhooks takes about fifteen. We do it with you on a call.
